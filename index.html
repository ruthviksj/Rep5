The statement p. 1

Hi, I'm Ruthvik.

Pull quote "I'd rather kill a feature than ship a metric." — from the long read, p. 02

An engineer who became a Product Manager because I got tired of building things nobody asked for. Most of what I do now is find the slow or manual step in a process and help rebuild the system around it. Currently Technical PM at Akteena; open to chats about what's next.

I trained as an Electronics & Instrumentation engineer at RVCE Bengaluru1. Built telematics for EVs at Log9 Materials. Did competitive analysis on the storage portfolio at Dell. Crossed over into product at Rang De in fintech — and stayed in product since.

There's a thread, if you're looking for one: I tend to end up wherever the process is slow, manual, or breaking in a way no one's quite owning end-to-end.
